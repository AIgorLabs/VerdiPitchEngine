## [**VerdiPitchEngine**] Release v0.3.0

> [!ABSTRACT] Executive Summary
> We've engineered a seamless symphony of sound transformation, ensuring your cherished music library remains perfectly organized and harmonious, even as VerdiPitchEngine elevates its pitch.

### 🌟 What's New & Why It Matters
AI.gorLabs is thrilled to unveil a monumental leap forward in your audio transformation journey! With this release, we've removed the friction from pitch conversion, allowing you to effortlessly elevate your music to 432 Hz directly within your existing library. Imagine a world where your meticulously curated playlists, play counts, and cherished metadata remain perfectly intact, even after a profound sonic upgrade. VerdiPitchEngine now performs its magic so discreetly, your favorite music management systems will never know the difference, ensuring an uninterrupted, harmonious listening experience.

### 🛠️ Key Enhancements
- **Seamless Library Integration with Metadata Preservation**: Experience the future of sound transformation as VerdiPitchEngine now converts your tracks to 432 Hz directly within their original location, seamlessly moving the 440 Hz originals to a hidden backup. Crucially, we've implemented advanced timestamp cloning, ensuring your music management systems (like Roon) perceive no change, preserving all your beloved playlists, listening history, and meticulously organized library exactly as you left them.
